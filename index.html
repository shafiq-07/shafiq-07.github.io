Base CampWhere it starts

Grown at altitude, engineered with new tools.

I grew up in Chipurson Valley — a remote, glacier-fed corner of Hunza, Gilgit-Baltistan, where the landscape is beautiful and unforgiving in equal measure.

Watching that terrain shift — floods, seismic tremors, glacial retreat — made infrastructure feel personal, not theoretical. That's why nearly every project I take on circles back to hazard-prone mountain regions, and why I don't limit myself to traditional civil engineering methods — I bring in Python, machine learning, and satellite data wherever they sharpen the answer.

I'm currently in my seventh semester of Civil Engineering at NUTECH, Islamabad, ranked #1 in my class with a 3.8/4.00 CGPA. Twelve years in scouting built the discipline and leadership underneath all of it, and the Amal Academy Fellowship sharpened how I turn that into organized community impact.

Yishkuk GlacierChipurson Valley · GEE Dashboard

Interactive GLOF Risk Monitoring Dashboard

Built an interactive Glacial Lake Outburst Flood (GLOF) risk monitoring dashboard in Google Earth Engine for Yishkuk Glacier, in my home valley. Using the Sentinel-2 archive (2019–2026), it computes NDWI to track seasonal meltwater and lake-surface-area trends, with a before-after slider to visually assess glacier and lake extent changes over time.

RC Multi-Storey BuildingStructural · Seismic

Nonlinear Seismic Performance Analysis

Full nonlinear seismic analysis in ETABS: modeled plastic and fiber hinges, ran pushover analysis to trace the building's capacity curve, then nonlinear time-history analysis against PEER ground motion records to assess real performance levels rather than relying on linear code-based approximations.
